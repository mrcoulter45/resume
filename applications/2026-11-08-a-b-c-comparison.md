## Evaluation method

Each A and B was read as a **recruiter** (first 15–30 seconds: title, role fit, core terms, legibility) and as a **technical hiring manager** (depth, complexity, ownership, transferability, and credible execution). A C was proposed only where it could combine distinctive strengths **without sacrificing** important posting requirements or introducing unsupported claims.

The weighted rubric: role relevance 25%; depth/excellence 25%; transferability 15%; differentiation 15%; recruiter/ATS clarity 10%; credibility/concision 10%. Each factor was scored from 1 to 5 and normalized to /100. The detailed role reviews contain the full factor-by-factor scores and reasoning.

A and B were kept intact. All three C résumés are separate Markdown revisions, with source mapping in adjacent `strategy.md` files. The original résumé content and proof constraints remain the source of truth.

## What the comparison establishes and does not establish

- Strongest **two-layer upside**: Hightouch's AI-product and AI-developer-productivity openings, plus Netflix N-Tech, because evidence of successful technical-domain transfers reinforces the posted role.
- Strongest **direct-fit advantage**: narrowly scoped TV test infrastructure, visualization/analytics product engineering, and NVIDIA EDA infrastructure, where proof of the core technical work is more valuable than adding unrelated breadth.
- Real gaps remain: customer-marketing AI product delivery, broad cloud-agent fleet operations, enterprise LLM launches, TV device-fleet orchestration, GraphQL/cloud-spend analytics and NVIDIA-scale cluster operations. Wording cannot create absent experience.
- Scores are one analytical assessment, not independent reviewer consensus, calibrated ATS scores, interview probabilities or proof from actual application results. For empirical learning, track recruiter response by *role*, submitted revision, date, level and channel; do not send competing versions simultaneously to the same requisition.
- This work did not change employer job captures, earlier A/B variants, campaign ranking or application status. **No applications were submitted.**
- Before sending, verify current posting and compensation (> $286k **actual total compensation** requirement), compile traced selections where needed, run the `career.py` validation/build/audit workflow and render + visually inspect PDF/DOCX files. Markdown-only revisions have not passed that pipeline.

## Practical next step

Use the **recommended** version above as the *draft of record* for each requisition; perform a final claim audit, 1–2-page export and visual inspection before applying. Capture any follow-up recruiter feedback and interview outcomes without assigning causality to an uncontrolled comparison.
