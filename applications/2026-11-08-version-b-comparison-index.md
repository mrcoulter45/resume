## Review rubric

Direct job relevance (25%), engineering excellence and depth (25%), demonstrated transferability (15%), differentiation (15%), recruiter/ATS clarity (10%), credibility and concision (10%). These are judgment weights, not validated interview probabilities. Review both a rapid recruiter scan and a technical hiring-manager read; retain the better résumé or selectively synthesize a hybrid only after comparison.

Six real employer-specific roles; the fictional example is excluded. The earlier versions, selections, job captures and campaign files are unchanged. These Markdown variants have not been through `career.py validate/build/audit` or rendered DOCX/PDF QA.
