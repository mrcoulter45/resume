## Core responsibilities

- Build user-facing developer tooling for Netflix's AI Platform supporting model creation, experimentation, evaluation and deployment.

- Build reliable, reproducible ML development workflows and develop interfaces for complex distributed systems.

## Employer qualifications

- Strong software engineering and user-focused platform/SDK/developer tooling for ML practitioners.
- Excellent Python and one of Scala, Java or C++.
- Experience alongside modern, large-scale ML services and understanding the model lifecycle, including training, experiment tracking and deployment.
- Familiarity with orchestration/tracking/features systems such as Metaflow, Airflow, Kubeflow or MLflow.
- Broad working ML concepts.
- 5+ years full-time experience with a **bachelor's and master's degree** or a **PhD**, as written in the posting.
- Preferred: model or LLM development, agentic systems, cloud (especially AWS) and significant open-source contributions.
